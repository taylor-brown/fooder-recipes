# Shrimp Fried Rice

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 30 min total

**Per serving:** 400 kcal · 33 g protein · 45.4 g carbs · 10 g fat · 3.8 g fiber

`weeknight` `asian` `one-pan` `uses-leftovers`

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1.5 cups dry brown rice, cooked and chilled
- 2 cups frozen peas and carrots
- 3 large eggs, beaten
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 green onions, sliced
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Use day-old cold rice. Fresh rice steams and goes mushy — this is the whole trick.
2. Scramble eggs in the pan, remove. Sear shrimp 2 minutes per side, remove.
3. Fry rice in oil over high heat, undisturbed 2 minutes at a time, until it crackles.
4. Add peas and carrots, garlic, soy sauce. Return shrimp and egg, toss. Finish with sesame oil and green onion.
