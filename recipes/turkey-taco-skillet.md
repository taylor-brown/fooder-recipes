# Turkey Taco Skillet

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 30 min total

**Per serving:** 635 kcal · 53.9 g protein · 58.2 g carbs · 22.5 g fat · 13.5 g fiber

`weeknight` `one-pan` `high-protein` `mexican` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 93/7 ground turkey
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 heaping tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 cup shredded cheddar, shredded
- 1 heart romaine, shredded
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Brown turkey in oil over medium-high, breaking it up, 6-8 minutes. Don't drain 93/7 — there's little fat and it carries the spice.
2. Add onion and garlic, cook 3 minutes. Stir in chili powder and cumin and toast 30 seconds.
3. Add beans, tomatoes and corn. Simmer 8 minutes until thickened.
4. Top with cheese, cover 2 minutes to melt. Serve over shredded romaine with yogurt.
