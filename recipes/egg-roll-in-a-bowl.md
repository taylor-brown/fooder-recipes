# Egg Roll in a Bowl

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 25 min total

**Per serving:** 443 kcal · 35.3 g protein · 41.7 g carbs · 15.4 g fat · 4.7 g fiber

`weeknight` `asian` `one-pan` `low-carb` `fast`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 93/7 ground turkey
- 1 lb bag coleslaw mix
- 3 carrots, shredded
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 4 green onions, sliced
- 1.25 cups dry brown rice *(optional)*
- 1 tbsp sriracha *(optional)*

## Method

1. Brown turkey over medium-high, 7 minutes, breaking it up.
2. Add garlic and ginger, 1 minute.
3. Add coleslaw mix and carrot with soy sauce. Toss 4-5 minutes — stop while there's still crunch.
4. Off heat, add sesame oil, green onion and sriracha. Serve as-is or over rice for the teens.
