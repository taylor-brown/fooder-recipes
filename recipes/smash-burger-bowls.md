# Smash Burger Bowls

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 25 min total

**Per serving:** 329 kcal · 38 g protein · 7.5 g carbs · 15.8 g fat · 2.1 g fiber

`weeknight` `high-protein` `low-carb` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 93/7 ground beef
- 2 hearts romaine, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup pickle chips
- 3/4 cup shredded cheddar
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 4 tsp dijon
- 2 tbsp ketchup

## Method

1. Whisk yogurt, dijon, ketchup and a spoonful of pickle brine into burger sauce.
2. Form beef into 12 loose balls. Smash hard onto a screaming-hot dry skillet, 90 seconds, flip, 45 seconds.
3. Top each with cheese in the last 30 seconds.
4. Pile romaine, tomato, onion and pickles in bowls, add patties, drizzle sauce.
