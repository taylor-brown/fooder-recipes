# Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowls

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 40 min total

**Per serving:** 458 kcal · 37.6 g protein · 57.2 g carbs · 8.9 g fat · 5.5 g fiber

`asian` `weeknight` `teen-favorite` `high-protein`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken thighs, cubed
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 4 tsp rice vinegar
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 1.5 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 heads broccoli, florets
- 4 carrots, sliced on the bias
- 1.5 cups dry brown rice
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds *(optional)*

## Method

1. Start rice. Whisk soy sauce, honey, vinegar, garlic, ginger and cornstarch with 1/4 cup water.
2. Sear chicken in a hot pan until browned and cooked through, 8-10 minutes. Remove.
3. Steam broccoli and carrot in the same pan with a splash of water, 5 minutes.
4. Return chicken, add sauce, simmer 2 minutes until it glazes. Serve over rice with sesame seeds.
