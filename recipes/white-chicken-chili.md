# White Chicken Chili

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 45 min total

**Per serving:** 733 kcal · 69 g protein · 99.3 g carbs · 8.1 g fat · 22.9 g fiber

`one-pot` `high-fiber` `high-protein` `make-ahead` `cold-weather`

## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb chicken breast
- 2 cans cannellini beans, drained
- 2 cans diced green chiles
- 2 onions, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 3/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 lime, juiced
- small bunch cilantro, chopped *(optional)*
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Soften onion and garlic in oil, 5 minutes. Add cumin and oregano, toast 30 seconds.
2. Add broth, chiles and whole chicken breasts. Simmer covered 18 minutes.
3. Remove chicken and shred. Mash about a cup of the beans into the pot to thicken it — no cream needed.
4. Return chicken with remaining beans, simmer 5 minutes. Off heat, stir in yogurt and lime. Never boil after the yogurt goes in or it will split.
