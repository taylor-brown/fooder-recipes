# Pork Tenderloin with Roasted Sweet Potato and Brussels

Serves 6 · 15 min active · 45 min total

**Per serving:** 413 kcal · 38.2 g protein · 36.1 g carbs · 13.7 g fat · 8.5 g fiber

`sheet-pan` `high-protein` `high-fiber` `weekend`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb pork tenderloin, trimmed
- 6 medium sweet potatoes, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 lb brussels sprouts, halved
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp dijon mustard
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 tsp rosemary
- 1.5 tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425F. Toss sweet potato and brussels with 2 tbsp oil and salt; roast 15 minutes.
2. Rub pork with dijon, garlic, rosemary and remaining oil. Sear all sides in a hot skillet, 6 minutes total.
3. Nestle pork onto the sheet pan. Roast 18-22 min until pork hits 145F.
4. Rest pork 10 minutes before slicing — cutting early loses the juice that keeps lean pork from going dry.
