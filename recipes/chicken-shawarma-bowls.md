# Chicken Shawarma Bowls

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 50 min total

**Per serving:** 461 kcal · 39.7 g protein · 43.4 g carbs · 14.4 g fat · 5.1 g fiber

`mediterranean` `high-protein` `meal-prep` `weekend`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken thighs
- 1.25 cups Greek yogurt
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 2 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2.5 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cucumbers, diced
- 3 tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1.25 cups dry brown rice

## Method

1. Marinate chicken in half the yogurt, half the lemon, garlic and all spices — 30 minutes minimum, overnight is better.
2. Heat oven to 425F. Spread chicken on a sheet pan, roast 25 minutes until charred at the edges.
3. Thin remaining yogurt with remaining lemon for the sauce.
4. Rest and slice chicken. Build bowls with rice, cucumber, tomato, onion and sauce.
