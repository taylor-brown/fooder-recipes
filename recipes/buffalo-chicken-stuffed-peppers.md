# Buffalo Chicken Stuffed Peppers

Serves 6 · 25 min active · 55 min total

**Per serving:** 407 kcal · 41.7 g protein · 40.6 g carbs · 8.3 g fat · 4.9 g fiber

`high-protein` `teen-favorite` `make-ahead`

## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb chicken breast, cooked and shredded
- 6 large bell peppers, halved and seeded
- 1/2 cup buffalo sauce
- 2/3 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 cup dry brown rice, cooked
- 2 stalks celery, finely diced
- 1 cup part-skim mozzarella, shredded
- 3 green onions, sliced

## Method

1. Heat oven to 400F. Arrange pepper halves cut-side up in a baking dish, roast 15 minutes to soften.
2. Mix shredded chicken, rice, celery, buffalo sauce and yogurt.
3. Fill peppers, top with mozzarella.
4. Bake 20 minutes until bubbling. Scatter green onion. Rotisserie chicken makes this a 30-minute meal.
