# Chicken Burrito Bowls

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 40 min total

**Per serving:** 768 kcal · 56.5 g protein · 97.2 g carbs · 18 g fat · 16 g fiber

`mexican` `high-protein` `high-fiber` `meal-prep` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1.5 cups dry brown rice
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1.5 cups frozen corn
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 heart romaine, shredded
- 3/4 cup shredded cheddar
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 lime, juiced
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Rub chicken with oil, cumin, chili powder and salt. Sear 5-6 min per side, rest, then chop.
2. Cook rice; stir lime juice and chopped cilantro into it off the heat.
3. Warm beans and corn together in the chicken pan, scraping up the browned bits.
4. Build bowls: lime rice, chicken, beans and corn, romaine, salsa, cheese, yogurt.
