# Peanut Chicken Noodles

Serves 6 · 25 min active · 35 min total

**Per serving:** 504 kcal · 39.6 g protein · 58 g carbs · 12.4 g fat · 5 g fiber

`asian` `weeknight` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
- 10 oz brown rice noodles
- 6 tbsp natural peanut butter
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 tbsp honey
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 10 oz sugar snap peas
- 3 carrots, julienned
- small bunch cilantro, chopped *(optional)*
- 2 tsp sriracha *(optional)*

## Method

1. Cook noodles, drain, rinse cold so they don't clump.
2. Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, lime, honey, garlic, ginger and 1/3 cup hot water into a pourable sauce.
3. Sear chicken 6-8 minutes. Add pepper, snap peas and carrot, cook 4 minutes.
4. Toss noodles and sauce into the pan. Finish with cilantro and sriracha.
