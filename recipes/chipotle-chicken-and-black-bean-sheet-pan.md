# Chipotle Chicken and Black Bean Sheet Pan

Serves 6 · 15 min active · 45 min total

**Per serving:** 584 kcal · 47.3 g protein · 71.9 g carbs · 12.2 g fat · 15.6 g fiber

`sheet-pan` `mexican` `high-fiber` `weeknight`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken thighs
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 5 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 red onion, wedges
- 3 tbsp chipotle in adobo, minced
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lime, juiced
- small bunch cilantro, chopped *(optional)*

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425F. Toss sweet potato and onion with half the oil, roast 15 minutes.
2. Toss chicken with chipotle, cumin and remaining oil.
3. Add chicken to the pan, roast 22 minutes until 165F.
4. Scatter beans over in the last 5 minutes to warm. Finish with lime and cilantro.
