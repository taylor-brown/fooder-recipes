# Greek Chicken Bowls

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 45 min total

**Per serving:** 523 kcal · 42.3 g protein · 49.5 g carbs · 17.2 g fat · 5 g fiber

`mediterranean` `high-protein` `meal-prep` `high-fiber`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1.5 cups dry brown rice
- 2 cucumbers, diced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2/3 cup crumbled feta, crumbled
- 1.25 cups plain Greek yogurt
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/3 cup olives, halved *(optional)*

## Method

1. Toss chicken with oil, half the lemon juice, oregano, half the garlic, salt and pepper. Marinate 20 minutes if you have it.
2. Start rice. Meanwhile mix yogurt with remaining garlic and lemon for tzatziki-style sauce; stir in half the cucumber.
3. Sear chicken over medium-high 5-6 min per side until 165F. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.
4. Build bowls: rice, chicken, tomatoes, remaining cucumber, red onion, feta, olives, sauce.
