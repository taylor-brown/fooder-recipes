# Steak Tacos with Lime Slaw

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 30 min total

**Per serving:** 434 kcal · 36.1 g protein · 33.6 g carbs · 18.3 g fat · 8.3 g fiber

`mexican` `weeknight` `teen-favorite` `fast`

## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb sirloin steak
- 12 corn tortillas
- 1 bag coleslaw mix
- 3 limes, juiced
- bunch cilantro, chopped
- 1/3 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil
- 1 avocado, sliced *(optional)*

## Method

1. Rub steak with oil, cumin, chili powder, salt. Sear 4 minutes per side for medium-rare. Rest 8 minutes.
2. Toss coleslaw mix with lime juice, yogurt and cilantro.
3. Char tortillas directly over a burner or in a dry skillet.
4. Slice steak thin against the grain. Build tacos with slaw and avocado.
