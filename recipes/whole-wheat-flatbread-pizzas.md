# Whole Wheat Flatbread Pizzas

Serves 6 · 15 min active · 30 min total

**Per serving:** 380 kcal · 32.5 g protein · 43.6 g carbs · 9.8 g fat · 6.2 g fiber

`fast` `teen-favorite` `weeknight` `customizable`

## Ingredients

- 6 whole wheat flatbreads
- 1.25 cups marinara
- 2 cups part-skim mozzarella, shredded
- 14 oz cooked chicken breast, shredded
- 1.5 bell peppers, thinly sliced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced *(optional)*
- 1.5 tsp italian seasoning
- 2 oz arugula *(optional)*

## Method

1. Heat oven to 450F. Toast flatbreads bare on the rack 3 minutes — this stops them going soggy under the sauce.
2. Top with marinara, chicken, vegetables, mozzarella and seasoning. Everyone builds their own.
3. Bake 8-10 minutes until the cheese blisters.
4. Scatter arugula over after baking. The night that keeps teenagers on board with the whole system.
