# Beef Chili

Serves 6 · 20 min active · 60 min total

**Per serving:** 850 kcal · 70.7 g protein · 103.4 g carbs · 18.8 g fat · 25.1 g fiber

`one-pot` `high-fiber` `make-ahead` `cold-weather` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 93/7 ground beef
- 2 cans kidney beans, drained
- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- 1 small can tomato paste
- 2 onions, diced
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2.5 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 2 cups beef broth
- 3/4 cup shredded cheddar *(optional)*

## Method

1. Brown beef hard in a dutch oven, 8 minutes. Remove.
2. Cook onion and pepper 6 minutes. Add garlic, chili powder, cumin and tomato paste; cook 2 minutes until the paste darkens.
3. Return beef with tomatoes, beans and broth. Simmer uncovered 35-40 minutes.
4. Better the next day. Makes a natural leftovers night midweek.
