# Oven Chicken Parmesan

Serves 6 · 25 min active · 50 min total

**Per serving:** 648 kcal · 58.2 g protein · 66.5 g carbs · 17.9 g fat · 9.9 g fiber

`italian` `high-protein` `teen-favorite` `weekend`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb chicken breast, halved horizontally into 6 cutlets
- 1 cup panko
- 2/3 cup grated parmesan, grated
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 2.5 cups marinara
- 1.5 cups part-skim mozzarella, shredded
- 2 tsp italian seasoning
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil
- 10 oz whole wheat spaghetti
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425F. Toss panko with parmesan, italian seasoning and oil — oiling the crumbs first is what makes baked cutlets crisp.
2. Dip cutlets in egg, press into crumb mix. Set on a wire rack over a sheet pan so air circulates underneath.
3. Bake 18 minutes. Spoon marinara and mozzarella over each, bake 6 more.
4. Meanwhile boil spaghetti and steam green beans. Serve together.
