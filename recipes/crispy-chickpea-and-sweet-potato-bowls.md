# Crispy Chickpea and Sweet Potato Bowls

Serves 6 · 15 min active · 45 min total

**Per serving:** 649 kcal · 23.6 g protein · 92.5 g carbs · 22.7 g fat · 11.3 g fiber

`vegetarian` `high-fiber` `sheet-pan` `meal-prep`

## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and dried
- 6 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 bunch kale, stemmed and torn
- 1.5 cups dry quinoa
- 1/4 cup tahini
- 2 lemons, juiced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 cup feta, crumbled *(optional)*

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425F. Dry the chickpeas thoroughly — wet chickpeas steam and never crisp.
2. Toss chickpeas and sweet potato with 2 tbsp oil, paprika and cumin. Roast 30 minutes, tossing once.
3. Cook quinoa. Massage kale with remaining oil and a pinch of salt.
4. Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic and water into a drizzleable sauce.
5. Build bowls: quinoa, kale, roasted chickpeas and sweet potato, tahini sauce, feta.
