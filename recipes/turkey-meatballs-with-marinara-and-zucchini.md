# Turkey Meatballs with Marinara and Zucchini

Serves 6 · 25 min active · 45 min total

**Per serving:** 577 kcal · 45.4 g protein · 59.5 g carbs · 19.7 g fat · 8.3 g fiber

`italian` `high-protein` `teen-favorite` `make-ahead`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 93/7 ground turkey
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 3/4 cup panko
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan, grated
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp italian seasoning
- 1 jar marinara
- 3 zucchini, spiralized or half-moons
- 10 oz whole wheat penne
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Heat oven to 400F. Mix turkey, egg, panko, parmesan, garlic, seasoning, 1 tsp salt. Don't overwork it.
2. Roll 24 meatballs onto a lined sheet pan. Bake 18-20 minutes.
3. Boil pasta. In the last 2 minutes, drop zucchini into the same pot.
4. Warm marinara, add meatballs. Serve over the pasta-zucchini mix — the zucchini stretches the portion without stretching the calories.
