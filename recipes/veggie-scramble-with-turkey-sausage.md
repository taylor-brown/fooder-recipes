# Veggie Scramble with Turkey Sausage

Serves 6 · 15 min active · 20 min total

**Per serving:** 477 kcal · 38.3 g protein · 23.7 g carbs · 24.9 g fat · 4.1 g fiber

`breakfast-for-dinner` `fast` `high-protein` `low-carb`

## Ingredients

- 12 large eggs, beaten
- 1 cup egg whites
- 14 oz turkey breakfast sausage
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 5 oz baby spinach
- 1 onion, diced
- 3/4 cup shredded cheddar
- 6 slices whole wheat bread, toasted
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Brown sausage in oil, 5 minutes. Add pepper and onion, cook 5 minutes.
2. Add spinach, wilt 1 minute.
3. Pour in eggs and whites. Cook low and slow, folding gently — high heat is what makes scrambled eggs rubbery.
4. Off heat, fold in cheese. Serve with toast. The lowest-effort night in the rotation, and it still lands 35g+ protein.
