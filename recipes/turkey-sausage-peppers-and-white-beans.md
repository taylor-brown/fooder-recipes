# Turkey Sausage, Peppers and White Beans

Serves 6 · 15 min active · 35 min total

**Per serving:** 768 kcal · 60.9 g protein · 98.6 g carbs · 16.2 g fat · 24.8 g fiber

`weeknight` `one-pan` `high-fiber` `italian`

## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb italian turkey sausage, sliced
- 4 bell peppers, sliced
- 2 onions, sliced
- 2 cans cannellini beans, drained
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2 tsp italian seasoning
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil
- 5 oz baby spinach

## Method

1. Brown sausage slices in oil over medium-high, 5 minutes. Remove.
2. Cook peppers and onion in the same pan 8 minutes until softened and browned at the edges.
3. Add garlic and seasoning, 30 seconds. Add tomatoes and beans, simmer 8 minutes.
4. Return sausage, fold in spinach until wilted. Good on its own or over any leftover grain.
