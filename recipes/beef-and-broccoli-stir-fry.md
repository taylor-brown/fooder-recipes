# Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Serves 6 · 25 min active · 35 min total

**Per serving:** 486 kcal · 35.1 g protein · 49.8 g carbs · 16.3 g fat · 5 g fiber

`weeknight` `asian` `high-protein` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb flank steak, sliced thin against the grain
- 2 heads broccoli, florets
- 1.5 cups dry brown rice
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 4 green onions, sliced
- 3/4 cup beef broth

## Method

1. Toss sliced beef with 1 tbsp cornstarch and 1 tbsp soy sauce. Start rice.
2. Whisk remaining soy sauce, cornstarch, broth, garlic and ginger for the sauce.
3. Sear beef in a very hot pan in two batches, 90 seconds per batch. Remove. Crowding is what makes stir-fry gray.
4. Add broccoli and 1/4 cup water, cover 4 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender.
5. Return beef, pour in sauce, toss 1-2 minutes until glossy. Finish with sesame oil and green onion.
