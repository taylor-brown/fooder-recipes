# Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas

Serves 6 · 15 min active · 40 min total

**Per serving:** 390 kcal · 40.3 g protein · 33.6 g carbs · 10.9 g fat · 6.3 g fiber

`weeknight` `sheet-pan` `high-protein` `mexican` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced into strips
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 2 medium onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1.5 tsp smoked paprika
- 1.5 tsp kosher salt
- 1 lime, juiced
- 12 corn tortillas
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- small bunch cilantro, chopped *(optional)*

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425F. Toss chicken, peppers and onion with oil and all spices on a large sheet pan.
2. Spread in a single layer — crowding steams instead of browning. Use two pans if needed.
3. Roast 22-25 minutes, tossing once, until chicken reaches 165F and edges char.
4. Squeeze lime over the pan. Warm tortillas and serve with yogurt and cilantro.
