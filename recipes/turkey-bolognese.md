# Turkey Bolognese

Serves 6 · 25 min active · 55 min total

**Per serving:** 585 kcal · 45.1 g protein · 65.5 g carbs · 19 g fat · 10.6 g fiber

`italian` `make-ahead` `high-fiber` `teen-favorite`

## Ingredients

- 2 lb 93/7 ground turkey
- 12 oz whole wheat penne
- 2 cans crushed tomatoes
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 3 carrots, finely diced
- 3 stalks celery, finely diced
- 2 onions, finely diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp italian seasoning
- 2/3 cup parmesan, grated
- 5 oz baby spinach

## Method

1. Soften carrot, celery and onion in oil over medium, 10 minutes. This is the base — don't rush it.
2. Add garlic and turkey, brown 8 minutes. Stir in tomato paste, cook 2 minutes.
3. Add crushed tomatoes and seasoning. Simmer 25 minutes.
4. Boil pasta. Fold spinach into the sauce until wilted, toss with pasta, finish with parmesan.
